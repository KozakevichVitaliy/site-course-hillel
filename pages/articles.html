Fun facts about Cats

A cat (scientific name Felis catus) is a small, domesticated carnivorous mammal. It belongs to the family Felidae, which also includes wild animals like lions, tigers, and leopards. Known for their agility, flexible bodies, and sharp claws, they are often kept as pets

    #1 Super Senses & Anatomy
  • Unique Nose Prints: Cats are believed to be the only mammals that cannot taste sweetness. Because they are obligate carnivores, they lack the receptors needed to enjoy sugary foods.
  • Whisker Symmetry: Cats typically have exactly 24 whiskers arranged in four rows on their upper lip (12 on each side), which act as a radar to help them navigate tight spaces.
  • ndependent Ears: They can rotate their cone-shaped ears 180 degrees using 32 muscles to pinpoint the exact location of high-frequency sounds.
