Who are Cats?

A cat (scientific name Felis catus) is a small, domesticated carnivorous mammal. It belongs to the family Felidae, which also includes wild animals like lions, tigers, and leopards. Known for their agility, flexible bodies, and sharp claws, they are often kept as pets

    Biological & Behavioral Traits
  • Scientific Name: Felis catus.
  • Diet: Obligate carnivores; they require a meat-based diet.
  • Traits: They are renowned for their agility, highly developed night vision, retractable claws, and acute hearing.
  • Classification: Belong to the order Carnivora and the family Felidae.
    Common Classifications
  • House Cat:A domesticated companion pet.
  • Feral Cat: A domestic cat that has reverted to a wild state and lives without human contact.
  • Stray Cat: A domestic cat that was once owned but has been lost or abandoned.
  • Big Cats: Wild feline species (e.g., lions, jaguars, cheetahs).
